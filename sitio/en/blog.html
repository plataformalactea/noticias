Raw milk rises for the second month running to €0.474/litre in September

According to delivery declarations to the Spanish Ministry of Agriculture, the average price in Spain rose from €0.469/l in August to €0.474/l in September, after hitting the year’s low in July (€0.465/l).

Deliveries totalled 596,601 tonnes, up 1.4%, and from January to September they reach about 5,695 million kilos (+1.7%). By region: Galicia €0.455/l, Castile and León €0.487/l, Cantabria €0.496/l and the Basque Country €0.502/l.

Cheese solids (CS) = fat + protein (%). Price per point = price per litre ÷ CS; equivalent to the price per hectodegree ÷ 100. Figures without a direct source are calculated from the other two. There is no official EU average for goat milk: France, the EU’s largest producer, serves as the reference.

Trends and production

  • Nov 2026Spain: in August goat milk was paid €0.1317 per point of cheese solids (+12.9% year on year), with average cheese solids of 7.95% (€1.047/l). Compared with July, the price per point is down 0.7%: the rise per litre (+1.7%) comes from richer milk. Deliveries grow 1.2% (33.2 million litres), after the first-quarter fall.Source: Ministerio de Agricultura ↗
  • May 2026Spanish production fell 10.8% in the first quarter (88.1 million litres) and the number of dairy goat farmers dropped 6.2% in a year.Source: Interempresas ↗
  • Jul–Sep 2026France: goat milk collection grows 6% in May and cheese sales 2%; the price paid in Q2, €837/1,000 l with cheese solids of 7.13% (≈ €0.117 per point), is up only €5 year on year. Spain, by contrast, pays 18% more than a year ago.Source: Transformation Laitière ↗
  • Nov 2026The Canary Islands keep losing goats: from 210,593 head in 2015 to 199,285 in 2024.Source: eDairy News ↗

Signals to watch: Spanish prices at record highs, with supply starting to recover in August, and France with more milk and stable prices. If collection keeps growing this autumn, price pressure could show in 2027 contracts.

France publishes the price per season (October to September) without a cheese-solids breakdown. There is no official EU average for sheep milk.

Trends and production

  • Nov 2026Spain: in August sheep milk was paid €0.1125 per point of cheese solids (+6.4% year on year), with average cheese solids of 11.53% (€1.298/l). Compared with July, the price per point is down 0.8%: the rise per litre (+1.8%) comes from richer milk. Deliveries rise 1.2% (40.9 million litres).Source: Ministerio de Agricultura ↗
  • Jul 2026From January to May production fell 1.7% (215.8 million litres), while the average price rose 5.7% to €0.1109 per point of cheese solids.Source: eDairy News ↗
  • Sep 2026Castilla-La Mancha: ASAJA estimates that Manchega sheep milk output in 2026 will be 25% lower than two years ago and says prices are below production costs, putting Manchego cheese supply at risk.Source: Agronews Castilla y León ↗
  • May 2026France: the price paid in the 2024-2025 season was €1,291/1,000 l (+4.3%), with more milk collected and slightly lower fat and protein. The source does not publish cheese solids, so it cannot be converted to a price per point.Source: Réussir ↗

Signals to watch: Spanish prices are recovering after the sharp fall in 2025, but production keeps declining, especially for Manchega sheep. Tight supply supports prices heading into 2027 contracts, although Manchega farmers remain below cost.
