1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser to remember information about your visit. Some are necessary for the website to work and others are used, for example, to analyse how it is used.

2. Use of cookies on this site

This website does not use cookies or similar technologies: it sets no first-party or third-party cookies, uses no analytics or advertising tools and loads no content from other servers. That is why no cookie banner is shown.

If non-essential cookies are added in the future, your consent will be requested beforehand and this policy will be updated.

3. How to manage cookies in your browser

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge or others).

4. More information

For any questions about the use of cookies or the processing of your data, see our Privacy policy or write to us at comercial@plataformalactea.com.
