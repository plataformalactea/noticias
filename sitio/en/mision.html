Mission

Turning one plant's surplus into another plant's supply.

Plataforma Interprofesional Láctea helps dairy plants channel their surpluses to other plants that need to cover one-off requirements, at the best possible price and to the benefit of both sides.

How we work

One product, two plants that win.

1

Plant with a surplus

It has milk, cream, butter, powder or cheese it will not use and wants to sell it without losing value.

Environment

Fewer kilometres, fewer emissions.

The way we work in itself minimises the carbon footprint. We source the products each plant needs by applying criteria that save on transport and therefore reduce pollution: we offer our customers the closest possible origins.

To do this we work with more than 90 suppliers in different countries and choose the best transport options, making use of trucks returning empty between supplier and customer. We already meet 99 % of this goal.
