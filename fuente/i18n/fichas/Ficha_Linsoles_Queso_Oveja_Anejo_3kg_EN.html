Aged pure sheep’s cheese «Linsoles»

Linsoles brand or private label · wheel of approx. 3 kg
PRODUCTAGED RAW SHEEP’S MILK CHEESE 3 KG
INGREDIENTSRaw sheep’s milk, salt, rennet, stabiliser: calcium chloride, and lactic cultures. Inedible rind. On the rind: preservatives (E-202, E-235) and colour (E-160b(ii)). Origin of the milk: SPAIN.
ORGANOLEPTIC CHARACTERISTICS
ColourHoney
SmellCharacteristic of the cheese
TextureCharacteristic of the cheese
INTENDED USESuitable for everyone except infants, people allergic to milk and people intolerant to lactose.
DIRECTIONS FOR USETo serve, cut with a two-handled knife and leave in a covered cheese dish at room temperature for about 30 minutes. Ideal serving temperature 16 °C. Once cut, wrap in food film and keep at around 5 °C.
RECOMMENDATIONSOn receipt, remove the plastic film from the pallet and take the cheeses out of the cases so they can breathe; this helps preserve them.
BEST BEFORE365 days
CODINGPacking batch: MNNNAA (M = line no.; NNN = Julian day of packing; AA = calendar year)

TRANSPORT TEMPERATURERefrigerated at 1–8 °C.
STORAGEKeep in a cool, dry place.
PROCESSSolid product resulting from the enzymatic coagulation of raw sheep’s milk, pressed and aged for at least 270 days.
